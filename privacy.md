*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

This policy explains how DZLabs GmbH processes personal data when you visit dzlabs.ch or contact us. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/nDSG) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Controller

DZLabs GmbH, see the [Impressum]({{ '/impressum/' | relative_url }}) for address and contact details.

## What this website does not do

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and has no contact forms. Text fonts are served from our own domain; icons load from a CDN (see below).

## Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you visit a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested page and your browser type. GitHub uses this data to deliver the website and to keep it secure. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Content delivery networks

To display the website, your browser loads style sheets and scripts from these providers, which receive your IP address and browser data:

- Bootstrap and Popper from cdn.jsdelivr.net
- jQuery from code.jquery.com
- Font Awesome icons from cdnjs.cloudflare.com

These providers may process data outside Switzerland and the EU, including in the USA. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the Swiss-US and EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## Contact by email

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request. We keep this data as long as needed for the request and for statutory retention periods.

## Your rights

You can ask us for information about the personal data we hold about you, and you can ask us to correct or delete it, restrict its processing, or object to it. Where the GDPR applies, you also have the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Switzerland, that authority is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

To use these rights, contact us at the address in the [Impressum]({{ '/impressum/' | relative_url }}).

## Changes

We may update this policy when the website or the law changes. The current version is always on this page.
